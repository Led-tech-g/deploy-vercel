I'm Led Paulus Macalinga.

I'm a 2nd Year BSIT student specializing in Mobile & Web Applications. One of the reasons Ano Tara? exists is because cafés feel like art to me the lighting, the cup ceramics, the way a barista pulls a shot, the playlist spilling from a corner speaker. Every café is a tiny museum, and I wanted a place online where students like me can find them without endlessly scrolling.

I built this for the gen z café-hoppers, the after-class ayaan, and anyone who's ever asked their seb the question: "Ano, tara?"

my partner, the main reason I fell in love with café hopping

Our story

Ano Tara? started as a list in my Notes app. After every café we tried, I'd jot down the address, the bestseller, and a one-line vibe check. Eventually friends started DM-ing me asking "saan ulit yung cafe na sinabi mo?" — and I figured: why not put it all in one website?

So this is it. A love letter to third places. New cafés get added monthly. Reviews are real. Recommendations are mine. And the door is always open. Ano Tara?
